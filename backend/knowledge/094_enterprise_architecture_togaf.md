# Module 094: ITIL 4 Framework & IT Service Management (ITSM) for Industrial Cyber-Infrastructure

## Overview
ITIL 4 Service Value System (SVS) and Service Value Chain (SVC) applied to industrial IT/OT environments: Incident Management, Change Enablement, Service Level Management (SLA/OLA), and IT Asset Management (ITAM) for plant digital continuity.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF)} = \dfrac{\text{Total Operational Uptime}}{\text{Number of Incidents}}$$
$$\text{Service Availability} = \dfrac{\text{MTBF}}{\text{MTBF} + \text{MTTR}} \times 100\% \ge 99.99\% \quad (\text{Four Nines Availability})$$

## Industrial Case Study
Implementasi ITIL 4 Change Enablement pada sistem OT SCADA kilang petrokimia menekan kegagalan patch update hingga nol insiden downtime tak terencana.
