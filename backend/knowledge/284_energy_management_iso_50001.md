# Module 284: Water Footprint Assessment (ISO 14046) & Industrial Wastewater Treatment Engineering

## Overview
Water Footprint Assessment (WFA) under ISO 14046 quantifies the volumetric water consumption, water degradation footprint, and water scarcity footprint across industrial product life cycles. It covers Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD), Reverse Osmosis (RO) optimization, and wastewater recycling in manufacturing.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{Water Footprint}_{\text{product}} = \text{WF}_{\text{blue}} + \text{WF}_{\text{green}} + \text{WF}_{\text{grey}}$$
$$\text{WF}_{\text{grey}} = \dfrac{L}{c_{\max} - c_{\text{nat}}} \quad (\text{Volume air yang dibutuhkan untuk mengasimilasi polutan})$$
$$\text{Water Scarcity Footprint} = \sum_{i} \text{Consumptive Water Use}_i \times \text{AWARE}_i$$

## Industrial Case Study
Implementasi sistem Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) pada pabrik tekstil berkapasitas 20.000 m3/hari menghemat 82% konsumsi air bersih dan mematuhi baku mutu Permen LHK No. 16/2019.
