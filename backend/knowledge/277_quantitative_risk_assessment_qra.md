# Module 277: Consequence Modeling for Industrial Catastrophes: BLEVE, Vapour Cloud Explosions & Toxic Dispersion

## Overview
Consequence modeling methodologies for chemical and process plant risk management: Boiling Liquid Expanding Vapour Explosion (BLEVE), TNT Equivalency & Multi-Energy Cloud Explosion (VCE), Gaussian Plume & Heavy Gas (Britter-McQuaid / DEGADIS) toxic chemical dispersion.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{Thermal Radiation from BLEVE Fireball: } q = \dfrac{R^2 E F_{\text{view}} \tau_{\text{atm}}}{x^2}$$
$$C(x, y, z) = \dfrac{Q}{2 \pi u \sigma_y \sigma_z} \exp\left( -\dfrac{y^2}{2 \sigma_y^2} \right) \left[ \exp\left( -\dfrac{(z-H)^2}{2 \sigma_z^2} \right) + \exp\left( -\dfrac{(z+H)^2}{2 \sigma_z^2} \right) \right]$$

## Industrial Case Study
Pemodelan radius dampak ledakan tangki LPG 50.000 ton di kawasan kilang minyak untuk penetapan zona aman (buffer zone) dan evakuasi pemukiman.
