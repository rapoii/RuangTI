# Module 146: Axle Load Distribution Optimization & Center of Gravity Cargo Securing (EN 12195)

## Overview
Optimizing the 3D placement of heavy industrial freight inside intermodal containers and semi-trailers considering legal road axle load limits, vertical and lateral Center of Gravity (CoG) envelopes, and cargo lashing friction coefficients according to European Standard EN 12195.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$R_{\text{front}} = \sum_{i=1}^n \dfrac{w_i (L_{\text{wheelbase}} - x_i)}{L_{\text{wheelbase}}} \le R_{\text{front, legal limit}}$$
$$R_{\text{rear}} = \sum_{i=1}^n \dfrac{w_i x_i}{L_{\text{wheelbase}}} \le R_{\text{rear, legal limit}}$$
$$F_{\text{securing}} \ge m \cdot g (c_x - \mu \cdot c_z) \quad (\text{EN 12195 Longitudinal Restraint Force})$$

## Industrial Case Study
Optimasi penataan muatan gulungan baja 28 ton pada trailer 40 kaki mencegah over-axle penalty dan risiko rollover di jalan tol trans Jawa.
