# Module 287: Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) & Heavy Industry Decarbonization Techno-Economics

## Overview
Engineering techno-economics of Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage (CCUS) technologies: Post-combustion amine absorption, oxy-fuel combustion, mineral carbonation, and $CO_2$ enhanced oil recovery ($CO_2$-EOR). Levelized Cost of Carbon Abatement (LCCA) calculation.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{LCCA} = \dfrac{\text{CAPEX} \times \text{CRF} + \text{OPEX}_{\text{annual}} - \text{Revenue}_{CO_2 \text{ sales}}}{\text{Annual } CO_2 \text{ Avoided (Tons)}} \quad (\$/\text{ton } CO_2)$$
$$\text{Capture Efficiency} = \dfrac{\dot{m}_{CO_2, \text{captured}}}{\dot{m}_{CO_2, \text{inlet flue gas}}} \times 100\%$$

## Industrial Case Study
Studi kelayakan tekno-ekonomi instalasi unit Carbon Capture amine solvent pada kiln pabrik semen kapasitas 3.000 ton klinker/hari dengan target emisi bersih 2035.
