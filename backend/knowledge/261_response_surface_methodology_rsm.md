# Module 261: Mixture Experiments & Simplex Formulation Design in Industrial Chemical/Materials Engineering

## Overview
Specialized experimental design for mixture ingredients where the components are non-independent proportions summing to unity ($100\%$). Covers Scheffé Canonical Polynomials (Linear, Quadratic, Special Cubic), Simplex Lattice, Simplex Centroid, and Constrained Mixture Designs.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\sum_{i=1}^q x_i = 1, \quad x_i \ge 0$$
$$\hat{y} = \sum_{i=1}^q \beta_i x_i + \sum_{i < j}^q \beta_{ij} x_i x_j + \sum_{i < j < k}^q \beta_{ijk} x_i x_j x_k \quad (\text{Scheffé Special Cubic Model})$$

## Industrial Case Study
Optimasi formulasi polimer komposit 4 bahan baku (resin, serat kaca, pengisi kalsium karbonat, aditif UV) untuk memaksimalkan kuat tarik dan ketahanan impak.
