# Module 258: Design for Disassembly (DfD) & Remanufacturing End-of-Life Index

## Overview
Product design guidelines for rapid end-of-life disassembly: Snap-fit standardization, non-destructive fastener release mechanisms, material homogeneity, and Disassembly Time / Cost modeling to maximize circular economic value retention in remanufacturing.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{Disassembly Efficiency Index (DEI)} = \dfrac{\sum t_{\text{theoretical min disassembly}}}{\sum t_{\text{actual disassembly}}} \times 100\%$$
$$\text{Remanufacturing Profitability} = P_{\text{reman}} - C_{\text{core}} - \sum_{k} t_{\text{disassemble}, k} \cdot R_{\text{labor}} - C_{\text{clean/test}}$$

## Industrial Case Study
Redesain baterai traksi motor listrik dengan sistem penguncian modular DfD memangkas waktu pembongkaran sel dari 45 menit menjadi 4.5 menit.
