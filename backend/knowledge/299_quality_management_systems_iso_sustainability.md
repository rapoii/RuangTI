# Module 299: Integrated Management Systems (IMS: ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001) & PAS 99 Framework

## Overview
Integration of multiple international management system standards into a single unified corporate governance structure using High-Level Structure (Annex SL / ISO Harmonized Structure) and PAS 99 specifications: Joint internal auditing, consolidated risk registers, unified policy statements, and integrated corrective action workflows.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{IMS Integration Degree (IID)} = \dfrac{N_{\text{shared procedures}}}{N_{\text{total standard clauses}}} \times 100\% \ge 80\%$$
$$\text{Audit Cost Savings Index} = 1 - \dfrac{C_{\text{integrated audit}}}{\sum_{k=1}^M C_{\text{standalone audit, } k}}$$

## Industrial Case Study
Integrasi sistem manajemen mutu, lingkungan, K3, dan keamanan informasi (ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, 27001) pada industri semikonduktor memangkas biaya audit tahunan 46%.
