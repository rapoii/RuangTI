# Modul 428: Pengantar Teknik Industri (Introduction to Industrial Engineering), Sejarah Scientific Management, 10 Pilar IISE Body of Knowledge, dan Pendekatan Sistem Terintegrasi

## 1. Domain Disiplin Ilmu & Definisi Otoritatif IISE
**Teknik Industri (Industrial & Systems Engineering)** didefinisikan secara resmi oleh *Institute of Industrial and Systems Engineers* (IISE):

> *"Industrial Engineering is concerned with the design, improvement, and installation of integrated systems of people, materials, information, equipment, and energy. It draws upon specialized knowledge and skill in the mathematical, physical, and social sciences together with the principles and methods of engineering analysis and design to specify, predict, and evaluate the results to be obtained from such systems."*

## 2. Kronologi Sejarah & Tokoh Peletak Fondasi Teknik Industri

```
[Revolusi Industri Awal (1760-1850)]
  - James Watt (Mesin Uap), Eli Whitney (Interchangeable Parts)
                 |
[Era Manajemen Ilmiah / Scientific Management (1880-1920)]
  - Frederick Winslow Taylor: Time Study, Prinsip Manajemen Ilmiah, Standarisasi Kerja
  - Frank & Lillian Gilbreth: Motion Study, 17 Elemen Gerak Dasar Therblig, Ergonomi Kelelahan
  - Henry Gantt: Gantt Chart untuk Penjadwalan Produksi Visual
                 |
[Era Riset Operasi & Statistik Kualitas (1930-1960)]
  - Walter A. Shewhart: Statistical Process Control (SPC Chart) & Siklus PDCA
  - George Dantzig: Algoritma Simpleks Pemrograman Linier
                 |
[Era Toyota Production System & Lean Six Sigma (1970-2000)]
  - Taiichi Ohno & Shigeo Shingo: Just-in-Time, Eliminasi 7 Pemborosan (Muda), SMED, Poka-Yoke
  - Bill Smith & Bob Galvin (Motorola): Metodologi Six Sigma DMAIC
                 |
[Era Industri 4.0 & Rekayasa Sistem Cerdas (2010-Sekarang)]
  - Smart Manufacturing, Cyber-Physical Systems, AI/ML Analytics, dan Circular Economy
```

## 3. 10 Pilar Utama IISE Body of Knowledge (BoK)

1. **Work Design & Measurement**: Pengukuran kerja, waktu baku, MOST, studi gerakan, perancangan tata letak kerja.
2. **Operations Research & Analysis**: Pemrograman matematis, simulasi stokastik, teori antrian, teori permainan.
3. **Quality & Reliability Engineering**: SPC, DOE Taguchi, IATF 16949, FMEA, Weibull Reliability, Lean Six Sigma.
4. **Supply Chain & Logistics**: Perencanaan agregat, WMS, VRP routing, optimasi persediaan multi-eselon, S&OP.
5. **Ergonomics & Human Factors**: Biomekanik kerja, antropometri, beban kerja mental NASA-TLX, K3/HSE.
6. **Facilities Planning & Material Handling**: PTLF, SLP Muther, desain gudang ASRS, routing AGV.
7. **Manufacturing Systems & Automation**: Otomasi CNC, robotika industri, PLC/SCADA, ISA-95 MES, CAD/CAM.
8. **Engineering Economy & Management**: Analisis kelayakan investasi NPV/IRR, depresiasi, penetapan harga pokok produksi HPP.
9. **Information & Systems Architecture**: Basis data relasional, IoT industri, arsitektur enterprise TOGAF.
10. **Sustainability & Sociotechnical Systems**: Ekologi industri, LCA ISO 14040, dekarbonisasi pabrik, etika keinsinyuran.
