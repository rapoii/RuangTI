# Module 285: Industrial Symbiosis & Kalundborg Eco-Industrial Park Resource By-Product Synergy

## Overview
Industrial Symbiosis (IS) models geographic clustering of heterogeneous industries where one company's waste or by-product becomes another company's raw material or energy source. Covers material exchange networks, waste heat cascading, energy balance optimization, and eco-industrial park (EIP) governance under UNIDO guidelines.

## Mathematical Formulation
$$\text{IS Network Resource Savings Index (RSI)} = \dfrac{\sum_{i} \Delta M_{\text{virgin}, i} + \sum_{j} \Delta E_{\text{fossil}, j}}{\sum M_{\text{baseline}} + \sum E_{\text{baseline}}}$$
$$\min \sum_{u, v} C_{\text{piping}}(u, v) + C_{\text{treatment}}(u, v) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{Quality}_{u, v} \ge \text{Standard}_v$$
